# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: procurement and logistics managers at companies importing cargo into Ghana (mining and heavy industry, FMCG and retail, manufacturing, NGOs, healthcare, e-commerce). They arrive needing to know quickly whether Premium 1 can handle their shipment and what it will cost.

Secondary (served by inner pages, not the homepage's main pitch): shipping lines and vessel owners needing a ship agent; small traders.

## Product Purpose
Marketing site for Premium 1 Logistics LTD, a freight and logistics company based in Airport Residential Area, Accra. The homepage's job is to make an importing company request a quote. Success is a submitted quote request (`/quote`); phone and WhatsApp are secondary routes.

## Positioning
One team handles the whole import: freight forwarding, customs clearance at Ghana's ports, and inland delivery, with one named contact and costs and assumptions stated in writing. (Open decision: the user has not yet confirmed a sharper differentiator backed by evidence.)

## Operating Context
Services listed on the site: freight forwarding, ship agency, import and export handling, customs clearance, warehousing and distribution, inland transportation and hauls, door-to-door delivery, supply chain support. Ports named in site content: Tema and Takoradi. Regional corridor destinations named: Abidjan, Lomé, Lagos, Cotonou, Ouagadougou, Bamako.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js site with quote form, shipment tracking, insights articles, service detail pages, admin.
- Deployed via Git push to Coolify; never edited on the server.
- Plain language for buyers. Avoid insider metaphors ("the file", "the desk", "field edition") as headline copy.

## Evidence on Hand
As of 2026-11-11 the user confirmed that the following homepage content is **placeholder and must not be presented as fact**: the Kwame Asante testimonial and mining-equipment case study, the event calendar dates, the leadership names and photo (Kwesi Owusu, Akosua Mensah, Kofi Annan Jr.), and by extension the mock stats (15+ years, 50+ clients), licence refs ("GHA-XXXX (replace)"), client logos, phone and WhatsApp numbers in `src/lib/siteConfig.ts`.

No real testimonials, client names, figures, licences or team photos have been supplied yet. Future work must not fabricate them; leave room for real proof to be added.

## Product Principles
1. Say plainly what Premium 1 does, for whom, and what happens next.
2. Every section leads to one action: request a quote.
3. Promise only what the company controls (written quotes, named contact, updates); never invent track record.
4. Answer the buyer's practical questions (documents, timing, pricing) on the page.
